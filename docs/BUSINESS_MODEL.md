# TracePilot Product and Business Model
## Initial customer
Individual developers and small engineering teams that regularly compare
passing and failing automated test runs.
## Free community edition
- Local JUnit XML comparison.
- Failure transition detection.
- Normalized failure signature grouping.
- Standalone HTML reports.
## Proposed Pro edition
A one-time commercial license may include:
- Additional report formats.
- Historical comparison management.
- Configurable report branding.
- Advanced grouping and filtering.
- Convenient executable packaging.
## Validation before charging
1. Interview at least 10 developers about their debugging workflow.
2. Demonstrate the tool using reproducible test fixtures.
3. Compare its results against existing tools.
4. Ask qualified users to test it on real projects.
5. Seek paid pilot commitments before investing in premium features.
## Revenue assumptions
A $29 license is a hypothesis, not a validated market price.
Do not advertise unimplemented features or claim proven time savings.
Check payment-provider eligibility and payout availability in Kenya.
